WHAT THE GAME IS
- One ROM with Johto (Heart & Soul), Hoenn (Emerald maps) and Kanto (FireRed maps = first era).
  Heart & Soul's own Kanto is kept as a later era (time jump; proposed: Celebi time travel).
- All Gen 1-9 Pokemon; cries at lower quality; generic surf blob; each region has its own badges,
  Elite Four and champion; end game after all three; a Day Care per region.
- Story plan: one story starting in Kanto (Pallet); visit Johto and Hoenn; meet Gold and Ruby
  (and Sapphire) as characters. New game currently still starts in New Bark (to change).
- Region rule (honour system): until a region's E4 is beaten use only its generations:
  Kanto 1/4/7, Johto 2/5/8, Hoenn 3/6/9.
- Credits/permissions: Francis III (Emerald Extended Cut), BelialClover (Pokemon R.O.W.E.).
