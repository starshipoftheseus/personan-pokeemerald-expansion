# Story & world
## Premise

One story across four regions, starting in one place (decided 2026-10-05):

1. **Kanto (FireRed era)**: the game starts in Pallet Town. FireRed's story as the first chapter.
2. **Johto** and **Hoenn**: reached for a story reason still to be written, which involves meeting
   their starters' trainers. Gold (Johto) and Ruby (Hoenn) may appear as characters.
3. **Kanto, later era** (Heart & Soul's Kanto): after the time jump.

Each region keeps its own badges, Elite Four and Champion; the end game opens after all of them.

Regions are joined by routes (map connections), as Emerald Extended Cut does, not portals:
see `extended_cut_notes.md`. Exact links are still to be designed.

Open questions:
- The narrative reason to leave Kanto for Johto and Hoenn.
- Is travel free once a region is reached (open world), or gated by story / HMs?
- How the time jump happens, and how the later Kanto is reached.
